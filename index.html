What is JapanMust?

JapanMust is a knowledge project for travelers visiting Japan. We explain the things that confuse first-time visitors most: restaurant systems, menus, dishes, and everyday logistics. Every guide is written from real experience, and every dish explanation tells you what's actually in front of you — not a dictionary guess.

New guides and dishes are added regularly. If there's something you want explained, tell us.
