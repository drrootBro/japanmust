Does Japanese curry have dairy? Allergens in Japanese curry explained

JapanMust Guides · 4 min read · Updated November 2026

Japanese curry rice (カレーライス, karee raisu) is one of the most popular meals in Japan, and it is very different from Indian or Thai curry. It is a thick, mild, slightly sweet sauce made from a pre-made roux: flour cooked in fat with curry spices, sold in blocks. That roux is where most of the allergens are.

Quick answer: Japanese curry often contains dairy (milk powder, butter or cheese in the roux) and almost always contains wheat. Ask カレーのルーに乳成分は入っていますか？ (karee no ruu ni nyuuseibun wa haitte imasu ka?) and check the allergen chart.

What is in Japanese curry roux

  • Wheat flour (小麦粉): the thickener. Standard curry is not gluten-free.
  • Fat: often beef tallow, pork lard or palm oil.
  • Milk ingredients (乳成分): many brands add milk powder, butter, cheese or cream for richness.
  • Soy: soy sauce or soy powder is common.
  • Extracts and fruit: beef, pork or chicken extract, sometimes seafood extract, plus apple, banana or honey for sweetness.

How to eat curry safely in Japan

  • Ask for the allergen chart (アレルゲン表). Big curry chains and family restaurants usually have one, and some chains sell a curry made without the major allergens.
  • At a small shop, ask about the roux. The cook often knows the brand and can show you the package.
  • For packaged curry, read the allergen line: milk appears as 乳成分, wheat as 小麦. Some supermarkets sell roux labeled 特定原材料不使用 (made without the major allergens).

Frequently asked questions

Does Japanese curry have dairy?

Often, yes. Many Japanese curry roux blocks and restaurant curries contain milk ingredients such as milk powder, butter, cheese or cream. Some do not, so you have to ask about the specific roux.

Is Japanese curry gluten-free?

Almost never. Japanese curry roux is thickened with wheat flour, so standard curry rice is not safe for celiacs. Rice-flour roux exists but is rare in restaurants.

Is Japanese curry vegetarian?

Usually not. Even vegetable curry is often made with a roux containing beef or pork fat, meat extract or seafood extract. Ask for a vegetarian or vegan curry specifically.

What allergens are in Japanese curry?

Common ones are wheat, milk, soy and sometimes beef, pork, chicken, apple, banana, sesame or shrimp extract. Toppings add more: katsu adds egg and wheat, cheese curry adds milk.
