Soba and buckwheat allergy in Japan: what to avoid and what to say

JapanMust Guides · 4 min read · Updated November 2026

Buckwheat is among the allergens that can cause the most serious reactions in Japan, and soba is a national dish. Restaurants that serve soba handle buckwheat flour and boil noodles in shared water. If you have a buckwheat allergy, this is a place where the safest choice is often to eat elsewhere.

Quick answer: Avoid soba restaurants if your allergy is severe. Say そばアレルギーです (soba arerugii desu), watch for そば粉 (sobako, buckwheat flour), and remember that yakisoba and Okinawa soba are wheat noodles.

Why soba restaurants are risky

Even if you order a different dish, a soba shop shares air, water, tools and sometimes fryers with buckwheat. Common issues:

  • Shared boiling water for noodles, and shared strainers and tongs.
  • Soba-yu (そば湯), the cloudy cooking water often served as a drink after the meal.
  • Tempura fried in shared oil next to soba orders.
  • Udon and soba from one kitchen. Many casual noodle shops cook both, and udon can pick up buckwheat.

Which “soba” is not buckwheat

The word is confusing. 焼きそば (yakisoba), 中華そば (chuuka soba, an old name for ramen) and 沖縄そば (Okinawa soba) are wheat noodles, so on their own they contain no buckwheat. The risk is shared kitchens and equipment. Real buckwheat noodles are written そば or 蕎麦, and 十割そば (juuwari) means 100% buckwheat. Nihachi soba (二八そば) is 80% buckwheat and 20% wheat. Our gluten-free page covers that wheat side.

Other places buckwheat appears

Buckwheat tea (そば茶, sobacha), buckwheat cookies (そばぼうろ), sobagaki (そばがき), and galettes (ガレット). On packaged food, buckwheat is a mandatory-label allergen, and you may also see wording about a shared factory: 同じ工場で〜を製造しています. Our label guide decodes it.

Printable buckwheat allergy card

Show this before you enter or sit down. If the restaurant handles soba, thank them and leave.

アレルギー · ALLERGY

私はそば（蕎麦）アレルギーです。

I am allergic to buckwheat (soba).

そば（蕎麦）が入っている料理は食べられません。

I cannot eat any dish that contains buckwheat (soba).

だしやソース、調味料、揚げ油にも入っていないか、ご確認をお願いします。

Please also check the stock, sauces, seasonings and frying oil.

そば（蕎麦）に触れた調理器具や油は使わないでください。

Please do not use utensils or oil that have touched buckwheat (soba).

そばを扱っているお店ですか？

Even a small amount can be life-threatening.

Frequently asked questions

Is yakisoba safe for a buckwheat allergy?

Yakisoba is made with wheat noodles, not buckwheat, but the same kitchen may handle buckwheat. Ask “そばを扱っていますか？” first.

Is udon safe if I am allergic to soba?

Not automatically. Many noodle shops cook udon and soba side by side with shared water and tools, so ask.

Is soba always labeled on packaged food?

Yes. Buckwheat is a mandatory-label allergen, shown as そば in the allergen line.

What is soba-yu and should I avoid it?

It is the cloudy water used to boil soba, served as a drink after the meal. Avoid it entirely with a buckwheat allergy.
